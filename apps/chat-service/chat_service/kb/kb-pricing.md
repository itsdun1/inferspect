# How Pricing Works

Ollive doesn't publish a fixed rate card — every AI product carries a
different risk profile and we underwrite each policy individually. Premium
is driven by:

- **Annual revenue** and the share attributable to AI features.
- **Deployment surface** — internal tools, B2B SaaS, or consumer-facing all
  price differently.
- **Regulated industry exposure** — health, finance, hiring, housing each
  carry a multiplier.
- **Controls maturity** — documented evals, observability and logging,
  red-teaming, human-in-the-loop review, and incident response all reduce
  premium materially.

Most early-stage AI vendors price between $15k and $60k annually for a
$1M-$5M aggregate limit. Quotes are usually returned within five business
days of submitting the application at `ollive.ai/apply`.
