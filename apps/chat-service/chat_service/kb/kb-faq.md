# FAQ

**Can I get coverage if my AI is built on third-party foundation models (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini)?**
Yes. Ollive covers the *deployer* of an AI system. You don't need to own the underlying model. Underwriting will ask about your evals, guardrails, and incident response so we can price accordingly.

**Do you require SOC 2 or ISO 27001?**
Not as a binding condition for early-stage vendors, but they reduce premiums materially. We give meaningful credit for: published eval results, LLM observability (latency / hallucination tracking), human-in-the-loop on high-risk outputs, and a documented incident response plan.

**How quickly can a policy be bound?**
Standard flow is 5-7 business days from completed application to bound policy. Expedited binding is available for renewals or pre-vetted vendors.

**What's the typical limit?**
Most early-stage customers buy $1M-$5M aggregate. Series B+ vendors commonly buy $5M-$25M. Coverage stacks above your cyber and E&O policies — Ollive is the first $$ specifically for AI risk.

**Will my premium go down if I add evals and observability?**
Yes. We give credits for evaluation pipelines, ongoing observability (latency / output sampling / drift detection), and incident-response retainer agreements. Tell underwriting what you have in place — it directly reduces the rate.

**What jurisdictions does Ollive write in?**
US (all 50 states), UK, EU. Other jurisdictions on a case-by-case basis.
