# Filing a Claim

If you believe an event may trigger coverage, notify Ollive within seven
days of the customer becoming aware of the incident. Email
`claims@ollive.ai` with:

1. A short description of what happened (the AI output, the affected
   user(s), the alleged harm).
2. Any written complaint, demand letter, or regulator correspondence.
3. The conversation, request, or model trace identifiers if available —
   Ollive's adjusters can work directly from observability platform exports.
4. A point of contact on your legal or security team.

Within two business days a dedicated claims handler is assigned and an
incident-response panel firm is on retainer if you need outside counsel.
Defense costs are paid as incurred — you do not advance them.
