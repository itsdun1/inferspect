# What Ollive's policy does NOT cover

Ollive's AI liability policy is purpose-built but is not a catch-all. Common exclusions:

- **Bodily injury or property damage** from physical products (e.g., AI in autonomous vehicles, medical devices). These belong on general liability or product liability policies.
- **Pre-existing claims** — incidents the insured knew about before the policy's inception date.
- **Intentional misconduct** — knowingly deploying a model to deceive, defraud, or harass.
- **Unencrypted training data at rest** without basic controls — Ollive treats this as gross negligence.
- **Crypto, gambling, or sanctioned-jurisdiction use cases** — outside underwriting appetite.
- **Cyber events not specific to AI** (ransomware, BEC, supply chain compromise of non-AI assets). A standard cyber policy still applies.

If you're unsure whether a specific risk is covered, the policy schedule lists exclusions explicitly and an Ollive underwriter (work@ollive.ai) can walk you through edge cases before binding.
