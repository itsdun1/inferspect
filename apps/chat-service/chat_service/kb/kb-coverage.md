# What Ollive Covers

Ollive's policy bundles six AI-specific coverages into one contract:

- **Hallucination & output liability** — third-party claims arising from
  incorrect, misleading, or harmful AI outputs (e.g. an AI support agent
  promises a refund that doesn't exist). Covers legal defense and settlement.
- **Algorithmic bias & discrimination** — claims alleging discriminatory
  outcomes from AI decision-making in hiring, lending, housing, or insurance.
- **Copyright & IP claims** — infringement, trade-dress, and trademark claims
  arising from content the customer's AI generates.
- **AI regulatory investigations** — defense costs and covered fines under
  emerging AI laws such as the EU AI Act and California's AI Safety Act.
- **Sensitive data disclosure** — confidential or regulated data revealed
  unintentionally by an AI system.
- **AI incident response** — forensics, public-relations, and remediation
  costs after a covered AI incident.
