# Who Should Buy Ollive

Ollive is built for enterprise AI vendors — companies whose product *is* the
AI, or whose product depends materially on an AI feature. That includes:

- AI-native SaaS startups (copilots, agents, generative tools).
- Established software vendors that have shipped a major AI feature
  (chatbots, summarisation, recommendations, code generation).
- AI infrastructure providers (model gateways, RAG platforms, evaluation
  tools) whose customers are themselves AI builders.
- Vertical AI in regulated sectors — fintech, insurtech, healthtech,
  legaltech — where the cost of a wrong output is concretely large.

If your sales team is being asked "what happens if your AI gives a wrong
answer and our customer sues us?" on every enterprise deal, Ollive is for
you. Coverage doubles as a sales accelerant: enterprise buyers close faster
when they trust your AI is insured.
