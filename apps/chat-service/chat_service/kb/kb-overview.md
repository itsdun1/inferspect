# Ollive — AI Liability Insurance for AI Vendors

Ollive is a specialty insurance provider for companies that build and sell AI
products. Traditional cyber and E&O policies now carry blanket AI exclusions,
leaving AI vendors exposed precisely where their risk lives. Ollive fills that
gap with a single policy purpose-built for modern AI risk.

Coverage spans six core categories: hallucination and output liability,
algorithmic bias and discrimination, copyright and IP claims, AI regulatory
investigations and enforcement, sensitive data disclosure, and AI incident
response (forensics, crisis comms, remediation). Customers are enterprise AI
vendors who want to reduce procurement friction, close enterprise deals
faster, and answer the AI liability question on every modern security review.
