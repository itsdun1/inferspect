# Getting a Quote

Getting an Ollive quote takes about thirty minutes of your time:

1. **Apply online** at `ollive.ai/apply`. The application asks for company
   basics, a description of your AI product, annual revenue, and a short
   set of controls questions (evals, observability, human review,
   incident response).
2. **Schedule a 30-minute underwriting call** with one of our AI risk
   engineers. The call is technical, not sales — we want to understand the
   model, where it sits in your stack, and the failure modes you've already
   thought about.
3. **Receive a quote** within five business days. Quotes are good for
   thirty days; bind coverage any time before they expire.

There is no charge to apply, and an Ollive underwriter is happy to review
your AI risk posture even if you ultimately don't bind a policy.
